

# AI Usage Declaration

This document describes how AI tools were used during the development of the Reliable Data Transfer over UDP project.

## Purpose of AI Assistance

AI tools were used as supporting resources during project development to improve understanding, debugging, testing, analysis, and documentation.

## Areas of AI Assistance

AI assistance was used for:

- Understanding networking concepts related to UDP and reliable data transfer.
- Clarifying Stop-and-Wait, Go-Back-N, and Selective Repeat ARQ behavior.
- Assistance with debugging and troubleshooting compilation or runtime issues.
- Suggesting test scenarios for packet validation, duplicate packets, and invalid acknowledgements.
- Interpreting experimental observations and recorded CSV results.
- Assisting with comparison and analysis of protocol behavior.
- Supporting graph generation and presentation of experimental data.
- Improving the clarity and organization of project documentation, including the README and final report.

## Implementation and Verification

The project implementation, experimentation, and integration were carried out by the team.

AI-generated suggestions and explanations were reviewed and checked against the project code, test outputs, and recorded experimental results. Experimental results documented in this repository are based on the team's recorded observations.

AI assistance was used as a supporting resource and not as a substitute for understanding the implemented protocols or evaluating the project.

## Responsibility

The team takes responsibility for the correctness, functionality, testing, and final submission of the project.
